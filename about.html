About Windstorm Services

Windstorm Services LLC is an independent game studio and publisher based in Shamokin, Pennsylvania. Right now it's one developer building Whirled: Second Wind because the original meant enough to bring back.

This is a self-funded, fan-driven project — not a work-for-hire studio, and not affiliated with Three Rings Design or Grey Havens.

Whirled: Second Wind is an independent, fan-made revival built from the open-sourced Whirled codebase. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Three Rings Design or Grey Havens. All credit for the original world goes to the people who built and played it.
