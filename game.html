What Whirled: Second Wind is

A social virtual world: you get an avatar, a room of your own, and a city full of other players' rooms to visit. Here's what's actually working, what isn't yet, and what's planned.

Working today

  • Custom avatars, carried over from the original item system
  • Player-owned rooms you can decorate and furnish
  • Visiting other players' rooms
  • Friends lists and in-room chat
  • The in-game economy — earning and spending coins
  • Buying, selling, and trading items

Not in yet

  • Parlor games and mini-games embedded in rooms
  • Costume remixing / advanced item creation tools
  • Groups and group-run spaces
  • Trophies and achievements
Coming later

What's planned

Mobile client

The desktop browser client is the priority right now. A mobile client is planned once that's solid — not started yet.

Community SDK

A toolkit for making your own rooms, items, and games inside Second Wind, the way creators could in the original. This comes after the core world is stable.
