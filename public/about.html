About the studio

About Windstorm Services

Windstorm Services LLC is an independent studio based in Shamokin, Pennsylvania, making our own apps and games. Right now it's one developer.

What we make

Couchpilot is a free Android remote for Android TV and Google TV, built because phone remotes shouldn't come with ads, subscriptions or tracking.

Whirled: Second Wind is a fan-made revival of the classic Whirled virtual world, built because the original meant enough to bring back.

How we work

Everything here is self-funded and built in the open. We'd rather ship something small that treats people well than something big that doesn't: no dark patterns, nothing collected that an app doesn't need, and accessibility designed in from the start.

We're not a work-for-hire studio, and Whirled: Second Wind is not affiliated with Three Rings Design or Grey Havens.
