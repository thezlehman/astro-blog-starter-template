Log 06 — Community

Built with the people who remember it

This project only makes sense as a community effort. Here's where that happens, and how to get involved.

Discord

The Discord is where development updates, screenshots, and test builds go out first — ahead of anything posted here.

Join the Discord →

Help test it

Once the browser client is ready for outside eyes (see the roadmap), testing rounds will be announced in Discord first. Joining the waitlist also gets you an email when that opens up.

Later

Creator content

The original Whirled was built on what its players made — rooms, items, and games created by the community. Second Wind is planning a community SDK to bring that back: a way to build and submit your own rooms, items, and games. That comes after the core world is stable (see the what's coming section). If that's something you'd want to build for, the Discord is the best place to say so.

Whirled: Second Wind is an independent, fan-made revival built from the open-sourced Whirled codebase. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Three Rings Design or Grey Havens. All credit for the original world goes to the people who built and played it.
