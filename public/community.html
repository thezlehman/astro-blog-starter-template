Log 06 — Community

Built with the people who remember it

This project only makes sense as a community effort. Here's where that happens, and how to get involved.

Discord

The Discord is where development updates, screenshots, and test builds go out first — ahead of anything posted here.

Join the Discord →

Help test it

Once the browser client is ready for outside eyes (see the roadmap), testing rounds will be announced in Discord first. Joining the waitlist also gets you an email when that opens up.

Creator content

What creators can do today — and what's coming

The original Whirled was built on what its players made. There's a first, basic version of that back already: you can upload your own art as an item and, if you list it for coins, earn a share every time someone buys it.

A fuller toolkit — for building whole rooms and games, not just single items — is still ahead, and comes after the core world is stable (see what's coming section). If that's something you'd want to build for, the Discord is the best place to say so.
