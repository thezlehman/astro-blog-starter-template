Why it exists

A remote you don't have to pay to keep

Too many phone remotes look free, then lock the buttons behind a subscription, or pretend to be casting apps. Couchpilot is a remote, it's free, and it says so.

What it does

  • A round D-pad wheel with a big OK button; hold a direction to keep moving
  • Volume, mute, power, Back, Home and Menu
  • Play/pause, rewind and fast-forward
  • Touchpad mode: swipe to move, tap to select
  • Type on your TV with your phone's keyboard
  • Open Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Disney+ and more in one tap
  • Your phone's volume buttons can control the TV
  • Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tiles

How it treats you

  • Free. No ads, no subscription, no trial, no in-app purchases.
  • Private. No account, no tracking. It only talks to your TV on your Wi-Fi.
  • Accessible. Every control works with TalkBack; high-contrast mode, four button sizes and a left-handed layout.
  • No gesture-only actions. There's always a button.

Before you install

Will it work with my TV?

Works with

  • TVs and streaming boxes running Android TV or Google TV
  • Chromecast with Google TV, onn., Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Sharp and others
  • Your phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi network

Doesn't do

  • Casting or screen mirroring (it's a remote)
  • The original Chromecast (without Google TV), Nest speakers, or TVs with only "Chromecast built-in". No phone remote can control those. Couchpilot tells you if it finds one.
  • Some TVs don't open some apps when asked

Couchpilot collects nothing. See the privacy policy for exactly what stays on your phone.
