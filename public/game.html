What Whirled: Second Wind is

A social virtual world: you get an avatar, a room of your own, and a city full of other players' rooms to visit. Here's what's actually working, what isn't yet, and what's planned.

Working today

  • Custom avatars, built from a curated set of looks
  • Player-owned rooms you can decorate and furnish
  • Visiting other players' rooms
  • Friends, mail, and in-room chat
  • Groups and parties
  • The in-game economy — earning and spending coins
  • Buying, selling, and trading items
  • 37 playable games, solo and multiplayer — quick board games up through real-time multiplayer arenas
  • Uploading your own art as an item, and earning a share when someone else buys it

Not in yet

  • Costume remixing — recoloring and reskinning outfits the way the original allowed. We looked into this in real depth; the original format needs more reverse-engineering work before it's possible.
  • Trophies and achievements
  • A fuller creator toolkit for building whole rooms and games, not just single items
Coming later

What's planned

Mobile client

Already underway — the same tech as the browser client, rebuilt natively. Room browsing, chat, and social features (mail, friends, parties) work there today. Games are just getting started: one is playable so far, with the rest of the 37 to follow.

Community SDK

A fuller toolkit for building whole rooms and games, not just uploading single items the way you can today. This comes after the core world is stable.
