How we build

The rules we hold ourselves to

Honest by default

No dark patterns and no surprise charges. Our apps say plainly what they do — and what they don't.

Private

We collect only what an app genuinely needs. Couchpilot collects nothing at all.

Accessible

Screen readers, high contrast, large controls and left-handed layouts are part of the plan, not an afterthought.

Small and reachable

One developer, working in the open. Email us and you'll hear back from the person who built it.

Questions or feedback?

Bug reports, ideas, press — it all reaches the same inbox.
