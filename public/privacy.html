Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy covers everything operated by Windstorm Services LLC ("we," "us"):

  • Couchpilot, our Android remote-control app for Android TV and Google TV
  • The windstormservices.us website
  • Whirled: Second Wind, its website and, once available, its client and services

Each has its own section below, because they handle data very differently. The commitments in "What we never do" apply to all of them.

1. Couchpilot (Android app)

Couchpilot collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service, and no servers of ours that it talks to.

What stays on your phone

  • Your TVs: the name, network address and public key of each TV you pair with.
  • Your settings: theme, button size, the apps you pinned, and similar choices.
  • A pairing key: a cryptographic key created on your phone in Android's secure Keystore. It proves to your TV that this phone is the one you paired, and it can't be copied off the phone, even by the app.
  • A crash report, only if the app crashes. It's saved on your phone and shown to you the next time you open the app, with a button to copy it if you choose to send it to us. It's never sent automatically.

This data is excluded from Android backups and never leaves your phone.

Network use

Couchpilot only connects to devices on your local network:

  • It looks for TVs using local network discovery (mDNS).
  • It connects to the TV you choose over an encrypted (TLS) connection, to send remote-control commands and receive things like the current volume.

It makes no connections to the internet.

Permissions

  • Network access, to reach your TV.
  • Nearby devices / local network (Android 17 and later), which Android requires for any app that talks to devices on your home network.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or use Settings → "Forget all TVs and reset pairing key". To remove your phone from a TV's list of remotes, use the TV's own settings.

2. The windstormservices.us website

Hosting logs: this site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may log standard technical data (IP address, browser, request timing) as part of normal operation.

Spam prevention: the waitlist form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which may process technical data (such as your IP address and browser signals) to verify you're human. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

3. Whirled: Second Wind

Waitlist: if you submit your email to join the waitlist, we store that email address with our email provider (currently Buttondown) so we can notify you about the project.

Game accounts: the Whirled: Second Wind client and account system aren't available yet. Before they launch, we'll update this policy to describe what they collect and how it's stored.

4. What we never do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use third-party ad-tracking networks.
  • Couchpilot contains no ads or analytics of any kind.

5. Children's privacy

Couchpilot doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your choices

  • Waitlist: to be removed, or to ask what data we have about you, email hello@windstormservices.us.
  • Couchpilot: we don't hold any data about you. Everything is on your phone and can be deleted as described in section 1.

7. Changes to this policy

We'll update the date above when this policy changes. For Couchpilot, changes ship with the app update they apply to.
