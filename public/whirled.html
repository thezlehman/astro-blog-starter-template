Why we're doing this

I played Whirled when it was still running. When Three Rings shut it down and later open-sourced the code, that codebase sat there for years: a whole virtual world, public, and mostly untouched. Second Wind is me picking it back up: getting it running without Flash, cleaning up what's broken, and building it back into something people can actually log into again.

It's one developer right now, working in the open, moving at the pace of someone doing this because they want it to exist, not on a launch deadline.

What's in it so far

Status: getting ready for testers. The core browser client — rooms, avatars, friends, trading, the in-game economy — is working. Testing rounds and a mobile client are next.

Working today

  • Custom avatars, carried over from the original item system
  • Player-owned rooms you can decorate and furnish
  • Visiting other players' rooms
  • Friends lists and in-room chat
  • The in-game economy — earning and spending coins
  • Buying, selling, and trading items

Not in yet

  • Parlor games and mini-games embedded in rooms
  • Costume remixing / advanced item creation tools
  • Groups and group-run spaces
  • Trophies and achievements

Coming later: a mobile client, once the browser version is solid, and a community SDK for building your own rooms, items, and games — the way creators could in the original.
